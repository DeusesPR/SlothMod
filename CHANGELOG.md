# Changelog

All notable changes to **SlothMod** are listed here. The format follows [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/) and the project uses [semantic versioning](https://semver.org/).

## 1.1.0

Major update for living RPG worlds, custom character and townsperson looks, themed HUD and inventory screens, and conflict-free modpacks across all six supported versions (`Fabric 26.3`, `NeoForge 26.3`, `Fabric 1.21.1`, `NeoForge 1.21.1`, `Fabric 1.20.1`, `Forge 1.20.1`). Existing `1.0.0` mod projects keep their original townspeople skins, town rosters and dungeon layouts unless you choose to update them.

- **Themed RPG HUD, Crystal Orbs & Matching Inventory Screens (All 6 Targets).** Mods with high health now show a clean single RPG Health Bar (`20` to `10,000+` HP with damage trails, absorption and an Armor badge) instead of rows of hearts, with optional animated liquid **Crystal Status Orbs**, themed hotbars, XP bars and toast banners, and matching pixel-art skins for your inventory, crafting table, furnace, chest, anvil, grindstone and stonecutter across six visual styles. Undead, construct and ghost characters automatically hide hunger drumsticks and show Mana or Soul Ward in their place, while living characters keep a Sustenance + Saturation bar alongside Mana.
- **Starting Origins (`Lv 1` to `10,000`), Class Trees, Live Disguises & 3D Regalia.** Players can pick between a `Lv 1` newcomer and a `Lv 100+` ruler in the same world from an auto-opening **Origin & Class Screen** with a live 3D character preview, exact HP/MP previews, single-holder ruler limits, and saved starter gear when switching origins. Characters can wear custom `64×64` layered skins, glowing eyes, capes, 3D crowns, pauldrons and floating orbs — plus **Disguise Signets** that change how strangers see and remember you while loyal companions still know who you are. *(Game Studio tunes level caps, starting levels, guild rules and skin toggles by hand; adding custom class or origin lists uses AI chat in 1.1.0.)*
- **All-Custom Living Towns, Multi-Kingdom Rule, Ritual Magic, Codex (`K`) & Modpack Checks.** Towns, farms, markets, caravans and sieges now spawn your mod's own sculpted creatures and `64×64` layered townspeople across fantasy races and outfits (no default villagers or iron golems), with clear street-level arrival spawns, wild-tree-free plazas that keep designed gardens and house timber intact, and calm single-channel town news. Added ranked Guild bounties, consent-based player Guild invites and Guild Halls, multi-town laws/taxes/elections/charters, multi-floor ring-gated dungeons, high-tier ritual magic with relic immunities and friendly-fire protection, shared world rumors (with automatic local PC AI detection when testing), an illustrated two-page **Codex (`K`)**, and **Verify Pack** modpack health checks with automatic keybind and HUD harmony.

## 1.0.0

First full release of **SlothMod** — a free Windows desktop studio for turning plain-English ideas (or zero-AI hand-crafted designs) into playable Minecraft Java Edition mods, custom worlds, ornate buildings, living colonies, RPG campaigns and complete survival overhauls.

- **World Studio & Live 60 fps 3D Voxel Terrain Sculptor (`$0` AI).** Roll continents and islands, paint custom biomes, rivers and world edges, turn a drawing or a real-world map area (OpenStreetMap + open elevation) into a playable Minecraft world, or open an existing Minecraft world save (`.mca`). Fly smoothly over stepped 3D voxel terrain with `1:1` single-block zoom, underwater seafloor depth, directional sun shadows, Terrain/Biomes view switching and optional **Pretty Light** mood lighting (sunrise, noon, sunset and night with glowing town lanterns). Sculpt mountains, valleys, flat plateaus, smoothed ridges, lakes, eight surface types and forests live across 3D and 2D with full `Ctrl+Z` / `Ctrl+Y` Undo and Redo — and drag-move, `90°`-rotate, height-nudge, copy, bulldoze or send existing buildings straight into **Build Studio**.
- **Build Studio, Ornate Facades, Room Furnisher & In-Game Blueprint Scrolls.** Design cottages, castles, cathedrals, solarpunk towers, skybridges and terraced hillside, cliff or lagoon scenes from plain English (with an optional 2-call 3D look review pass), a free floor-plan sketch, a reference photo, imported `.nbt` / `.schem` / `.litematic` schematics or `$0` starters. Dress buildings in 3D Gothic or Solarpunk facades, swap whole wood or stone families (or single blocks) while preserving stair and door orientation, furnish 16 room types automatically, place `512+`-block mega-structures, and give players craftable in-game **Blueprint** scrolls with ghost previews, `90°` turning, ground-up building and safe undo.
- **Theme Studio, 3D Sculpting, 3D Painting, Keyframe Animation & Sound/Music Maker.** Craft your mod's complete visual style in **Theme Studio** — tuning 12 colour swatches, surface materials, wear, glow and custom `8×8` pixel stamps on a live 6-card pixel-art sample sheet. Sculpt 3D props, furniture and weapons box-by-box (`1 px` or `1/4 px` snap, `±22.5°` / `±45°` angles) with UV-preserving handoff to **3D Paint & Grab** (`Scales`, `Crystal`, `Metal`, `Fur` and `Rune Glow` smart brushes synced live via `F3+T`), animate creatures on a `0–200` tick **Keyframe Timeline** with left/right limb mirroring and `.bbmodel` / `.animation.json` clip import, and generate 614 sound types plus seamless looping area and boss music across 8 styles.
- **Living Towns, Brain Studio & Self-Building Colonies (`$0` AI).** Give townspeople homes, beds, family names, daily schedules, gift preferences, witness-based guard law, unclamped personalities, episodic memories, relationships, patrol routes and squad orders in **Brain Studio** (with 12 `$0` starter minds, a live 24-hour Calm Day vs Storm & Raid simulator, and optional live AI companions). Found player-run **Colonies** with a Town Hall, Build Scepter, block-by-block builders (`Lv 1–3` upgrades), warehouses, couriers, worker huts, colonist traits and moods, painted stockpile/grow/bunker zones, a `1–4` manual work priority grid and dynamic Storyteller events in **Colony Studio**.
- **Whole-Game Studio, RPG Campaigns & Total-Conversion Gameplay.** Compose complete survival overhauls in **Game Studio** (or play and remix 8 `$0` **Instant Overhaul Worlds** from fresh spawn to boss victory). Add custom biomes, multi-biome dimensions, standing `4×5` portals, branching multi-room dungeons, branching dialogue trees with `d20` skill checks, camera cutscenes, quests, storylines, living world chronicles with rival warlord promotions and diplomacy, seasons and greenhouse crop rules, dynamic supply/demand markets and player stalls, town banks, laws and mayor elections, tabletop Game Master scepters and seeded multi-lane Tower Defense, creature Capture Orbs with multi-branch evolution and genetics, rideable 8-element dragons, multi-move bosses with breakable limbs, generation-2 guns, staffs and radial spell wheels, dodge rolls, timed parries, grappling hooks, wall-running, player scale/form morphs, local time-stop, shadow clones and domain arenas, moving multi-block ships, airships and elevators, conveyors, power grids, rail block signals and pressurized habitats, sight-cone stealth and Spirit Camera mysteries, playable 24-note instruments and NPC choirs, slash commands, advancement trees, custom game rules, villager professions, custom workstations and plank wood-set trees, **Visual Blueprints**, **Open `.jar` Mod** & `.zip` **Overlay Pack** export, full Java **Code** mode, and a built-in **Modrinth** mod and shader manager.
- **Safety, Privacy & Built-In Checking.** No accounts, no tracking and no telemetry; API keys stay in Windows Credential Manager and work with any cloud AI or local server (LM Studio / Ollama). Every build runs inside a sandbox copy with pre-build code, metadata and build-file safety checks, automatic in-game mod checks on `Fabric 26.3`, `NeoForge 26.3`, `Fabric 1.21.1` and `NeoForge 1.21.1`, a throwaway-server modpack compatibility check, version history, one-click Undo and a plain-English crash explainer.
- **Still being polished.** `Fabric 26.3` and `NeoForge 26.3` are the lead targets with real Gradle builds, dedicated-server verification, live client checks and automatic in-game mod checks; all six supported targets (`Fabric 26.3`, `NeoForge 26.3`, `Fabric 1.21.1`, `NeoForge 1.21.1`, `Fabric 1.20.1`, `Forge 1.20.1`) compile real `.jar` mods. Natural World Studio terrain generation, the newest dragon riding/camera features and some visual polish lead on `26.3`, while `1.21.1` and `1.20.1` receive features in batches with plain notes inside the app wherever a feature differs. Large multiplayer playthroughs across every new overhaul system are still being fine-tuned in real play. SlothMod is free and not paid-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a blue warning — click **More info**, then **Run anyway**. If something acts up, use **Report a problem** inside the app.
